社区介绍

Community Introduction

CloudRV Community（芯筑云原社区）作为OIF OpenStack RISC‑V SIG、CNCF中国RISC‑V兴趣组对外统一的生态品牌载体。面向Cloud+RISC‑V交叉领域，弥合云基础设施技术栈与RISC‑V架构适配缺口。

汇聚企业、科研院所、开源社区、开发者等产学研用多方力量，打通硬件算力、基础软件、云原生技术栈的协同链路。

围绕OpenStack、Kubernetes、容器、编排组件开展架构移植、性能优化、最佳实践沉淀，推动RISC‑V在服务器、数据中心、行业云等真实业务场景落地。

社区承担对外传播、技术共享、人才培育、生态共建职能，输出技术路线图、案例成果、技术文档，构建中立开放的Cloud+RISC‑V协同创新生态。

CloudRV Community is the unified external brand for OIF OpenStack RISC‑V SIG and CNCF China RISC-V Interest Group. Targeting the Cloud+RISC‑V cross domain, it bridges the adaptation gap between cloud infrastructure stacks and the RISC‑V architecture.

It brings together enterprises, research institutes, open-source communities and developers to connect hardware computing, system software and cloud-native stacks.

The community focuses on architecture porting, performance tuning and best practices for OpenStack, Kubernetes and container orchestration, accelerating RISC-V adoption in servers, datacenters and industry clouds.

CloudRV Community delivers technical roadmaps, case studies and documentation, fostering a neutral, open collaborative innovation ecosystem for Cloud+RISC-V.

CloudRVCon 大会 · 社区启动仪式

CloudRVCon · Community Launch Ceremony

在CloudRVCon芯筑云原：Cloud+RISC-V技术沙龙暨RISC-V SIG成立大会上，将同步举办RISC‑V SIG工作组成立暨CloudRV Community（芯筑云原社区）双启动仪式。

仪式将正式对外宣告：OIF OpenStack RISC‑V SIG、CNCF中国RISC‑V兴趣组正式成立，同时发布“CloudRV Community”作为两大工作组统一对外社区品牌，标志芯筑云原社区正式启动运行。

现场邀请指导单位、发起企业、科研机构代表共同上台启动，线下参会专家与线上开发者共同见证。仪式之后，CloudRV Community将作为对外窗口，持续开展技术研讨、代码协同、案例分享与生态合作，持续推进Cloud+RISC‑V技术适配与产业落地。

The CloudRVCon: Cloud+RISC-V Salon & RISC-V SIG Establishment Conference will host the dual launch ceremony for RISC‑V SIGs and CloudRV Community.

The ceremony officially announces the founding of OIF OpenStack RISC‑V SIG and CNCF China RISC-V Interest Group, and launches CloudRV Community as the unified public brand for both working groups.

Representatives from steering organizations, founding enterprises and research institutes will jointly kick off the community, witnessed by on-site experts and online developers. After the launch, CloudRV Community will serve as the public hub for technical workshops, code collaboration, case sharing and ecosystem cooperation, to drive Cloud+RISC-V technical adaptation and industry adoption.
